Why We Are Listening

Understanding the Field First

In this first phase, Nav Utkarsh is listening to the field — understanding the gaps, support needs, and opportunities in Integrated Natural Resource Management and Livelihood Development. We believe that effective land and resource governance improves productivity, food security, resilience, and income opportunities for rural communities, while empowering women, youth, and vulnerable groups.

Current evidence shows that CSOs often struggle not only with technical issues, but with deeper problems like long-term sustainability, leadership, strategy, and lack of customized capacity support. We want to change that.
