Professional Profile

A Journey of Impact

I have more than 30 years of experience as a geographer, focusing on rural development, watershed management, and sustainable livelihoods. I hold a PhD in watershed management and have dedicated my career to understanding how natural resources support biodiversity and help communities thrive.

I started out working directly on watershed research projects, where I learned to plan and analyse hydrology, soil, water, climate, agriculture, and socio-economic factor interactions at the local level. Later, I led several watershed and livelihood projects with NGOs and became part of the State-level Government Consortium IWMP – SNLA in Jharkhand.

I have held leadership roles in various NGOs, from Project Coordinator to Executive Director, and implemented the "Lakpati Kisan Smart Village" initiative with CInI – Tata Trust in Jharkhand.

For the past eight years, I have worked as a Senior Program Officer and Technical Advisor at the Bharat Rural Livelihoods Foundation (BRLF), leading large-scale high-impact watershed projects in West Bengal, Jharkhand, and Chhattisgarh in collaboration and convergence with state governments, NGOs, and funding agencies (CSRs). These projects have helped turn degraded areas into productive ecosystems.

My work bridges academic knowledge and practical solutions, with a focus on watershed management and livelihood security to build sustainable economic models. Over the years, I have grown from a researcher into a strategic leader, tackling natural resource management, environmental issues, climate resilience, and social and economic challenges in rural areas.
